Biography

Built To Move
The Floor

KAELO is an Argentine DJ whose passion for electronic music has been shaping dancefloors for more than 13 years. With a sound rooted in House and Tech House, enriched by Afro Tech influences, he has developed a distinctive style that blends groove, rhythm, and energy into carefully crafted musical journeys.

His performances are driven by a deep understanding of the dancefloor. Every set is built through precise track selection, smooth transitions, and an instinctive ability to read the crowd — creating an atmosphere that evolves naturally from warm, hypnotic grooves to powerful peak-time moments.

Throughout his career, KAELO has performed at well-known venues including Moscú, Creta, Bali, and Groove, consistently delivering professional performances that leave a lasting impression. His experience allows him to adapt seamlessly to clubs, private events, and festival environments while maintaining a signature sound that reflects his artistic identity.

Always focused on musical evolution, professionalism, and creating unforgettable moments, KAELO continues expanding his presence within the electronic music scene, building strong connections with audiences through every performance.
